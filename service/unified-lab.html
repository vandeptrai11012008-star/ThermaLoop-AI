04 / SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

What this solver does — and does not

Water is an incompressible fluid with fixed nominal properties. The steady pump/system operating point uses a pump curve and Darcy–Weisbach pipe losses. Two-stream heat exchangers use counter-flow ε-NTU. The transient model conserves heat across aggregate GPU, mixed coolant, and one room.

Not calibrated: no measured pump curves, GPU thermal impedance, facility loads, variable fluid properties, pressure transients, branched flow, stratification or CFD. Passing numeric residual tests does not establish real-world accuracy or safety.
